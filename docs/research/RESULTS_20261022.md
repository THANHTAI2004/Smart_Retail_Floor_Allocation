report; the report retains dispersion, intervals, failed/unexecuted counts,
paired comparisons and multiplicity adjustment where valid.

E08 mean process peaks are 141,258,752 / 141,811,712 / 144,121,856 /
154,166,886 bytes. OS polling can miss brief peaks; runtime depends on this
machine's concurrent load. These numbers are not cross-machine benchmarks.

E09 volatile/uneven arms all completed; nearest and exact produced the same mean
route of 5.7071 m. This is evidence of robustness on these generators, with no
route-policy advantage demonstrated. E10 repeats identical seeds twice; repeats
are averaged before inferential pairing rather than treated as independent data.

The intentionally impossible control configuration separately exercises greedy
search exhaustion versus MILP-proven infeasibility. These outcomes belong in the
failure report and are not converted into successful solutions.

passed `pip check`. All 19 pilot replays have identical input/result SHA-256 and
numerically equivalent results at absolute tolerance 1e-8 / relative 1e-9:
[clean comparison](../../experiments/reports/20261022-clean-comparison-02.json).

or a different solver/platform guarantee. Telemetry is intentionally outside the
