# Threats to validity

- Construct validity: T2 centroid distance differs from T4 walking distance; demand-weighted T3 capacity is not profit or service level. Stock scoring assumes demand means units per horizon.
- Internal validity: the common cumulative deadline favors early candidates; process startup/polling and uncontrolled machine load affect wall time. A shared time ceiling does not prove equal computation actually used.
- External validity: tiny synthetic instances, no calibrated crowd/picker data and no multi-store population. Five seeds are not five field studies.
- Statistical validity: small n, exploratory multiplicity and constant differences cannot establish general efficacy. Holm adjustment does not replace power or preregistration.
- Physical validity: shelf load is shared across faces; usable depth is modeled per face. Actual shelf specifications must validate this interpretation. Facings are fixed and there is no mixed SKU depth/height interleaving.
- Evidence validity: hashes prove identity, not authenticity, licensing or field accuracy. Source/dependency drift requires a new identity/campaign.
